# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

DevConnect is committed to a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for developers of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate professionally and provide constructive feedback.
- Respect privacy, consent, and personal boundaries.
- Accurately represent identities and contributions.
- Credit others' work and encourage collaboration.
- Keep discussions relevant and welcoming.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, threats, bullying, personal attacks, doxxing, impersonation, spam, and pressure to follow or promote accounts are not permitted.

## Moderation and enforcement

Maintainers may request changes, remove inappropriate contributions, or limit participation for violations of these standards. Actions will be based on the nature and severity of the conduct.

## Reporting concerns

Use a GitHub issue for non-sensitive concerns. Do not publish private or confidential reports in public issues. For sensitive concerns, contact maintainers privately through an officially published contact channel, if available.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository contributions and project-related discussions, including issues and pull requests.
